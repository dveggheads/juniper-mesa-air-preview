Privacy Policy

At Juniper Mesa Air, we respect your privacy. This policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your information when you submit a service request or communicate with us.

1. Information We Collect

When you submit an estimate or diagnostic inquiry, we collect your name, phone number, city, email address (if provided), and details describing your HVAC system or cooling issues.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely to respond to your inquiry, schedule an on-site visit, prepare an installation consultation, and communicate directly regarding your air conditioning services. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

3. Communications & Consent

By providing your phone number and email address, you consent to receive direct communications from Daniel Reyes at Juniper Mesa Air regarding your specific request. We do not use automated marketing robocalls or unsolicited text broadcasts.

4. Contact Us

If you have questions about your data or wish to update your contact preferences, please call us directly at (602) 555-0147.
